# Fitness scoring

Score how well the candidate's documented background matches the job.

## Method

1. Separate the JD's qualifications into **required** and **preferred** items.
2. Look for direct evidence for each item in the resume. Never assume a skill, credential, or experience that is not documented.
3. Give partial credit for clearly transferable or adjacent experience, but less than for a direct match.
4. Treat explicit numeric requirements literally, such as years of experience.
5. Weight required qualifications much more heavily than preferred ones.
6. Note significant gaps, but do not penalize a candidate merely for being overqualified.

## Score anchors

Use half-point increments.

- **9.0–10.0:** Meets all required qualifications and most preferred qualifications with highly relevant experience.
- **7.0–8.5:** Meets the required qualifications; some preferred items are missing or only partially supported.
- **5.0–6.5:** Meets most required qualifications; the gaps appear reasonably bridgeable.
- **3.0–4.5:** Misses several required qualifications or one major requirement.
- **0.0–2.5:** Poor match or a clear hard blocker.

Base the score only on the resume and JD supplied for this evaluation.
